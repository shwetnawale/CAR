# 🏎️ How to Play: The Self-Driving Car Game!

Welcome! In this game, you get to draw a racetrack and watch smart AI cars learn how to drive on it completely by themselves.

Here is exactly how to play, step-by-step:

### Step 1: Open the Game
1. Open your terminal (like PowerShell).
2. Type this to go to the game folder: `cd Path of ur folder wher the project is \CAR`
3. Type this to start the game and hit Enter: `python main.py`

### Step 2: Draw Your Racetrack!
When the screen opens, you are the artist!
- **Left Click & Hold:** Draw the black road for the cars to drive on.
- **Right Click & Hold:** Erase the road if you make a mistake.
- **Up & Down Arrows:** Press these on your keyboard to make your drawing brush bigger or smaller!

### Step 3: Start the Race
- When your track is finished, press the **SPACEBAR** on your keyboard to lock it in.
- Move your mouse to where you want the cars to start, and **Left Click** once. 
- Boom! 50 cars will instantly spawn and start trying to figure out how to drive!

### Step 4: Your Super Powers
While the cars are driving, you can press these secret buttons on your keyboard:
- **The Finish Line:** Put your mouse at the end of your track and press the **F** key. A giant Red Target will appear! The first car to touch it wins the race!
- **Fast Forward:** The cars might take a few tries to learn the corners. Press the **H** key to turn on "Hyper-Speed" and watch them train super fast!
- **Hide the Lasers:** Press the **V** key to hide the blue laser beams so you can just watch the cars drive.
- **Save the Winner:** If a car is doing really well, press the **S** key to save its brain!
- **Start Over:** Messed up your drawing? Just press **F5** to instantly wipe the board and start fresh!

### Step 5: Watch the Champion
When a car hits the Red Target, it wins! A Victory popup will appear. Press the **P** key to watch a perfect replay of that champion car driving the whole track flawlessly!
